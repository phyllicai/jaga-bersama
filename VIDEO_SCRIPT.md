# Demo video script (about 2:45)

The narration is generated with edge-tts (voice en-SG-LunaNeural) from `video-build/segments.json`. The screen recording is driven by `video-build/record.py` against the static demo, and `video-build/build.sh` mixes them into `demo.mp4`. There is no music.

| # | Time | Screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Empty app, header, family line | This is Jaga Bersama, which means caring together in Malay. Millions of Malaysian families share the cost of looking after an ageing parent across cities and even countries. One sibling lives nearby, pays for the clinic and gives up her weekends. The others want to help, but the money talk is awkward and the spreadsheet never gets updated. Jaga Bersama turns the family chat into fair, transparent PayPal payments. |
| 2 | 0:27 | Chips replay Aisyah (BM), Farid, Hakim, Hakim (中文); ledger fills | Siblings just type what happened, in English, Malay or Chinese. Aisyah took Mak to the clinic and paid the home nurse. Farid bought supplies from Singapore. Hakim paid for physio, and this message is in Chinese. The agent pulls out each amount, category and payer into a ledger, and logs Aisyah's fourteen hours of caregiving. |
| 3 | 0:50 | Month stats and split table | The split is deterministic code, not the language model. Each sibling has a weight, and caregiving time earns credit at fifteen ringgit an hour, so the sister doing the caring is not also paying the most. Shares always add up exactly to what was spent. |
| 4 | 1:07 | "Jom settle bulan ni", proposals card | When someone says settle this month, the agent proposes PayPal actions: an invoice for Farid, an invoice for Hakim, and a PayPal Payout to reimburse Aisyah. Nothing moves until a human approves. Payouts above five hundred ringgit need two approvals, and never only the person being paid. |
| 5 | 1:26 | Approve Farid invoice, simulate payment (webhook), Hakim pays via Checkout dialog | Approving Farid's invoice calls PayPal Invoicing: create, then send, with idempotency keys. Here Farid pays it. A PayPal webhook arrives, and the app re-fetches the invoice from PayPal instead of trusting the webhook body. Hakim prefers to pay straight away, so he uses PayPal Checkout, Orders version two, and his pending invoice is withdrawn so nobody is billed twice. |
| 6 | 1:51 | Two approvals release the payout, fee in the ledger, "Siapa belum bayar?" | Now the fund has money, so Farid and Hakim approve Aisyah's reimbursement through PayPal Payouts. PayPal's checkout fee is booked as a shared cost, so no one quietly absorbs it, and the payout never exceeds what the fund actually holds. Anyone can ask who hasn't paid, and get the answer from the ledger. |
| 7 | 2:10 | Audit trail and PayPal API calls | Every PayPal call is in the audit trail. The same tools are exposed through an MCP server, so any AI assistant can record costs and propose settlements, but the approve tool is never exposed to an AI. This demo runs on a mock with the exact PayPal sandbox response shapes. Add sandbox keys and the same code calls the real PayPal APIs. |
| 8 | 2:32 | Mobile width view | Jaga Bersama is open source under the MIT license. Fair shares, gentle reminders in the family's own language, and PayPal doing the money. Terima kasih. |
